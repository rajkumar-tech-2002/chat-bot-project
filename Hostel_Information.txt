UNIVERSITY HOSTEL INFORMATION 2026

1. Hostel Fees
- Single Room (AC): $5,000 per semester
- Double Shared Room (Non-AC): $3,000 per semester
- Triple Shared Room (Non-AC): $2,200 per semester
- Security Deposit (Refundable): $500 (One-time payment during admission)

2. Mess & Cafeteria Charges
- Standard Meal Plan (3 meals/day): $1,200 per semester
- Premium Meal Plan (includes evening snacks & weekend specials): $1,600 per semester
- Late night canteen is open from 11:00 PM to 3:00 AM on weekdays.

3. Hostel Rules & Curfew
- All students must return to the hostel premises by 10:30 PM.
- Visitors are strictly prohibited inside student rooms. Visitors can meet students in the designated Guest Lounge between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM.
- Intoxicants, smoking, and alcohol are strictly prohibited on campus.

4. Facilities Included
- High-speed 5G Wi-Fi in all rooms and common areas.
- 24/7 laundry service (coin-operated machines available on each floor).
- Gymnasium and indoor sports complex (free for all hostel residents).
- Weekly room cleaning services.

5. Allocation Process
- Hostel rooms are allocated on a first-come, first-serve basis.
- Freshmen (first-year students) are guaranteed residential housing if they apply before August 1st.
- Room change requests will only be entertained after the first month of the semester.
